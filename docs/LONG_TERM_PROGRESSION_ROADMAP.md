# Long-Term Progression Roadmap

Status: Stages 4 and 5 are implemented in the reviewed working source; collection expansion and external release gates remain open.

This roadmap deliberately avoids premium currency, real-time waits, timed exclusivity, forced resets, accounts, analytics, and social pressure. Delivered phases preserve local saves through staged migrations. A checked item means source-integrated with automated coverage; it does not mean exact-production, human accessibility, cultural, narrative, or balance approval.

## Phase 1 — Neighbourhood demand events and peak periods

- [x] Add seeded breakfast, lunch, tea, and evening demand profiles with readable advance notice.
- [x] Add eight local fictional neighbourhood events.
- [x] Let events change volume, budgets, preferences, party mix, and meal mix, never identity or health-condition incidence.
- [x] Show event modifiers during planning and explain the chosen or neutral result in the debrief.

Delivered contract: keyed selection is deterministic and RNG-neutral, at most one event occurs per period and two per day, event service choices are untimed, and dismissal applies the authored neutral outcome. Human narrative tone and long-session balance review remain release gates.

## Phase 2 — Seeded scenarios and constrained layouts

- [x] Author scenario definitions with an isolated map, seed, starting S$, unlock set, operating modifiers, and medal thresholds.
- [x] Add Narrow Aisles, Split Hall, Limited Cash, Menu Diversity, and Peak Throughput.
- [x] Keep challenge timing simulation-based and pause-aware.
- [x] Store independent scenario saves and best medals without changing the main centre.

Delivered contract: scenarios are reproducible, medal rewards are cosmetics only, bronze has a competent seeded strategy, and gold has at least two seeded strategies. Exact-production offline use and human difficulty calibration remain open.

## Phase 3 — Dish discovery, collections, and centre identity

- [ ] Add dish mastery based on service quality and customer preference matches.
- [ ] Unlock additional recipe-presentation variants and centre decorations through play, not random drops.
- [x] Add a local gallery with a clear source for scenario and mastery rewards.
- [ ] Add centre colour and signage themes that preserve contrast and cultural-review requirements.

Dependencies: existing stall mastery, content validation, save-schema extension policy, and human cultural/accessibility review.

Acceptance: collection completion has no missable entries or timed exclusivity; cosmetic choices never become dominant economic bonuses.

## Phase 4 — Customer groups and community stories

- [x] Add deterministic parties of up to six, member-level orders, shared seating needs, and readable party outcomes.
- [x] Add six recurring fictional regulars with persisted encounter counts and four-step story progress.
- [x] Keep behavioural and condition-aware needs independent of ethnicity, age, appearance, regular identity, event, and neighbourhood theme.
- [x] Provide a regular gallery and debrief summaries without voice or external content.

Delivered contract: one canonical routed party owns the queue footprint, seating is reserved atomically with an explained adjacent-table fallback, story progress never blocks core progression, and condition-aware needs are resampled independently each visit. Full lifecycle visual observation and qualified story/cultural review remain open.

## Phase 5 — Endgame mastery contracts

- [x] Add deterministic seven-day rotations with exactly three feasible breakfast choices or Skip.
- [x] Freeze accepted targets and validate templates against current unlock capacity.
- [x] Reward only titles or cosmetics, grant each reward once, and label completed-template practice replays.
- [x] Preserve earned medals, titles, and cosmetics across main-centre reset while providing a separately confirmed erase-all flow.
- [ ] Use post-launch human telemetry-free balance sessions to decide whether later contracts should scale from demonstrated throughput rather than authored validated targets.

Delivered contract: twelve templates cover high-level service, layout, menu, forecast, party, ledger, and Combo Board play. Automated feasibility is not a substitute for human engagement or difficulty review.
